The research method

How to use a Litbuy spreadsheet with intention.

A useful product link organizer should help you make a decision. Keep the information that changes your choice, and leave unverified details clearly marked.

When collecting Litbuy finds, distinguish an attractive reference image from evidence about the actual item being offered.

Start your category research ↗

  1. Choose the category and purpose

    Start with everyday sneakers, a warm layer, a matching set or a specific accessory. A focused shortlist is easier to compare than an unstructured fashion finds spreadsheet.

  2. Save the exact product link

    Record the listing, selected color, size and seller name. Add the date checked so an old note is not mistaken for current information.

  3. Check measurements and evidence

    Review the size chart, detailed photos, material description and return terms. If quality check photos are available, confirm they show the relevant item and variant.

  4. Compare the complete cost

    Separate item price from delivery, service fees and possible import charges. Confirm current platform rules and route availability before paying.

Keep the useful columns

A shortlist you can actually compare.

Use this structure for a Litbuy clothing spreadsheet, a Litbuy shoe spreadsheet or an accessories list.

Category Record in your sheet Question to resolve
Shoes Model reference, color, selected size, sizing notes and any available insole measurement. Does the sizing information match your foot and a shoe that fits you?
Hoodies, T-shirts & jackets Chest width, length, shoulder width, sleeve length and stated fabric composition. Is the fit based on garment measurements or only a size label?
Pants, shorts & underwear Waist range, rise, hip measurement, inseam, stretch information and return restrictions. Will the selected cut and measurements work for the intended use?
Sets & jerseys Included pieces, separate top and bottom sizing, fabric notes and print details. Are all pictured pieces included, and are personalization terms clear?
Headwear Circumference, crown depth, fastening and adjustment range. Does the adjustment range cover your measurement comfortably?
Jewelry & accessories Dimensions, weight if supplied, stated material, closure and care instructions. Are the material claims supported, and are relevant sensitivities addressed?

The item price is only one part of the budget.

Estimated total = item price + domestic delivery + service/payment fees + international shipping + applicable import charges. Some components may not apply. Keep the currency and estimate date visible, and replace estimates with confirmed amounts when available.

Clear answers

Before you follow a product link.

This Litbuy shopping checklist explains the scope of the guide and the information to verify independently before placing an order.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Litbuy spreadsheet means a product research sheet used to organize fashion links, categories, sizes, seller notes and estimated costs. A spreadsheet entry alone does not verify product quality, authenticity or availability.

Does this page provide an official Litbuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent editorial guide with links to CAFANS browsing categories. It does not provide an official Litbuy sheet, confirm a Litbuy integration or guarantee that a listed item can be purchased through Litbuy.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check the measurement units and whether the chart describes body measurements or garment measurements. Do not rely only on a letter size.

What belongs in a shopping cost estimate?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, service or payment fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges. Keep estimated amounts separate from confirmed charges.

Do the brand images confirm CAFANS stock or authenticity?

No. The images are editorial references for footwear, clothing, headwear and accessories. They do not confirm CAFANS inventory, product authenticity, brand authorization or an affiliation with the brands.
